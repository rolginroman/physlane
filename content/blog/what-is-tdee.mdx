## Why TDEE matters

**Total daily energy expenditure (TDEE)** is the sum of calories your body uses in 24 hours — at rest, through movement, and while digesting food. If you eat below TDEE on average, weight tends to fall; above it, weight tends to rise.

![Analytics illustration](/blog/what-is-tdee/hero.webp)

## The three buckets

Most models split TDEE into basal metabolic rate, activity, and the thermic effect of food. You do not need a perfect split to make progress — you need a number you can test against real weigh-ins.
